Introduction

My name is Hind Marzouq Alahbabi, and I am an 18-year-old university student. I move around during the day and walk as part of my university routine, but I do not currently follow a structured exercise plan. Most of my meals are prepared at home, and I have no food allergies.

Through this portfolio, I will explore ways to make my daily habits more consistent. My goals focus on regular walking, balanced meals, and taking short breaks to manage study-related stress. I have chosen changes that can fit around university without requiring a gym or complicated meal preparation.

This portfolio includes my SMART goals, a two-week fitness and meal plan, AI-supported nutrition and fitness activities, and a final reflection. In the AI activities, I check the results against recognised guidance before deciding how useful they are for me.
